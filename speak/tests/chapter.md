# A Short Walk

The morning was *cold* and clear, and the road ran down the hill toward the river.
We took the long way, past the old mill and the bakery that opens before the sun is up.

## The River

The water was high after the rain. A heron stood very still in the shallows, and
for a long time none of us said a word. Then the bird lifted, slow and heavy, and
was gone over the trees on the far bank.

- Bread from the bakery
- Tea in a flask
- A map we never opened

> We should do this every week, my brother said, and we all agreed.

## Home

By noon the clouds had come in and the wind turned. We walked back the way we had
come, tired and happy, and the kettle was on before we had our boots off. It was
the kind of day you remember for a long time without being able to say why. The
light, perhaps, or the quiet, or simply that nobody was in a hurry to be anywhere else.
